Model-car impact telemetry

ImpactIQ

An Arduino Uno and MPU-6500 detect sudden movement on a model car. The dashboard shows the event’s peak acceleration and inferred direction, alongside an interactive 3D view.

What it measures

The sensor captures acceleration at its mounting point. The project uses the strongest event and sensor axes to show impact strength and front/back/left/right direction.
